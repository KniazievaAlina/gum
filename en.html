About Us

LLC Trading House "Guild of Ukrainian Machine Builders" is a modern industrial enterprise founded in 2010. We specialize in the manufacturing, modernization, and repair of complex process equipment, as well as the fabrication of steel structures of any complexity. Combining innovative engineering solutions with high-quality materials from leading manufacturers, we provide industrial enterprises in Ukraine with reliable and durable products at all stages — from concept to finished product.

Key Areas of Activity
Equipment Manufacturing

Serial and custom manufacturing of mining and metallurgical equipment according to customer drawings or our own engineering designs.

Repair & Modernization

Overhaul and scheduled repairs of process equipment with deep modernization in accordance with modern technical requirements.

Turnkey Steel Structures

Design and manufacturing of steel structures for any application using high-quality rolled steel and components.

Industrial Engineering

Years of practical experience in cooperation with leading mining, metallurgical, and industrial enterprises in Ukraine.

Providing industrial enterprises with reliable, high-tech equipment for the development and reconstruction of Ukraine's infrastructure.

Partnership
Metinvest Holding LLC

Metinvest Group is an international group of mining and metallurgical companies owning assets in Ukraine, Italy, Bulgaria, the United Kingdom, and the United States, extracting ore and coal, producing coke, smelting steel, and manufacturing rolled products, pipes, and more. Group assets are managed by the parent company — Metinvest Holding LLC.
