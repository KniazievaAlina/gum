Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026.

This Privacy Policy defines the procedure for obtaining, storing, processing and protecting personal data of users of the website of LLC “TD “Guild of Ukrainian Machine Builders” (hereinafter — the “Enterprise” or “We”).

We respect your privacy and undertake to protect personal data in accordance with the Law of Ukraine “On Personal Data Protection”.

1. What information do we collect?

We may collect and process the following categories of data:

▶ Information you provide yourself:

▷ First and last name;
▷ Contact phone number;
▷ Email address;
▷ Message or request text.

▶ Technical data (collected automatically):

▷ IP address;
▷ Browser type and version;
▷ Operating system;
▷ Cookies (short files with data about site visits);
▷ Time and duration of the session on the site.

2. Purpose of personal data processing

We use your data exclusively for the following purposes:

▶ Processing and calculating the cost of orders (manufacture of metal structures, parts, major overhaul);

▶ Contacting you to clarify technical details or provide engineering consultation;

▶ Conclusion and performance of supply contracts or service agreements;

▶ Analysis of site performance to improve its usability and technical functioning.

3. Transfer of data to third parties

We do not sell, transfer or disclose your personal data or provided technical drawings to third parties, except in cases:

▶ When this is expressly provided for by the current legislation of Ukraine (at the request of law enforcement or judicial authorities);

▶ When this is necessary to fulfill your order (for example, transferring data to a transport/logistics company for delivery of finished equipment).

Separately about commercial secrecy:
All drawings, diagrams and technical specifications provided by you are considered confidential and are used exclusively for evaluation or performance of work by the engineering and design department of the enterprise.

4. Storage and protection of information

▶ We take all necessary organizational and technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

▶ Personal data is stored for the time necessary to achieve the purposes of its processing, or for the period determined by the current legislation of Ukraine.

5. Cookies

Our site uses cookies to analyze traffic and improve the resource. You can disable cookie storage in your browser settings at any time, however this may affect the correct display of some site functions.
